- **A28. A client that goes away in the middle of a sign-in leaves its draft.** LIMIT · reasoned.
  The daemon does not watch who called `Accounts.SignIn`. When the window is closed or
  `konedrivectl account add` is killed while the browser is open, the draft stays: a sign-in
  finished in the browser after that still adds the account, with nobody to open the folder picker
  or print its name, and otherwise the draft ends at the sign-in's timeout or at the next `SignIn`,
  whichever comes first. The other way round, a window that waits for `SignInFinished` has no
  timeout of its own: when the daemon stops in the middle (A30) no signal comes, and the window
  shows the sign-in as under way until Cancel is pressed, whose refused `CancelSignIn` ends the
  wait. Open.
