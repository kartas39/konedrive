- **A28. A client that goes away in the middle of a sign-in leaves it under way.** LIMIT ·
  reasoned. The daemon does not watch who called `Accounts.SignIn`. When the window is closed or
  `konedrivectl account add` is killed while the browser is open, the sign-in goes on: one finished
  in the browser after that still adds the account, with nobody to open the folder picker or print
  its name, and otherwise it ends at the sign-in's timeout, or at the next `SignIn` or
  `SetClientId`, whichever comes first. Nothing was made meanwhile, so nothing is left behind. The
  other way round, a daemon that stops in the middle (A30) sends no signal, and neither client
  waits for one: the window and `konedrivectl account add` each end with an error when the
  daemon's name leaves the bus. Neither has a timeout of its own for a daemon that stays on the bus
  and never answers: the window waits until Cancel is pressed, and `konedrivectl account add` has
  only its six minutes for the whole sign-in. Open.
