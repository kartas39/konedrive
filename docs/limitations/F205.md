- **F205. A removal that fails half-way leaves the account without its folder, and a failed
  add can leave its entry** (`daemon/manager.rs` `remove` and `add`, `sync/take_down.rs`
  `unretire`; quality finding `SY5`) — `Accounts.Remove` forgets the folder first, then deletes
  the sign-in, then takes the account out of `config.toml`. When the second or the third step
  fails, the account stays and works again (it takes a folder, a sign-in and a mode, and can be
  removed again), but the first step is not taken back: the folder is forgotten at the helper,
  its read-only lock is off, and a OneDrive folder's tree store is deleted, so the folder
  cannot simply be registered as it was. The files stay. A sign-in that was under way is given
  up, and when the third step fails the account is signed out as well. What follows:
  - Only the refusal says it (and the daemon's log): nothing is kept in `LastError`, so a client
    that drops the message of a failed `Remove` shows an account that lost its folder with no
    reason.
  - If `config.toml` cannot be written, the record of the folder cannot be taken out of it
    either (`persist_or_log` only logs; a plain Forget has the same weakness). The refusal
    says that the folder was forgotten and is still recorded; the folder is taken for
    registered again at the next registration or start, without its tree store.
  - A `config.toml` from which the account was taken by hand while the daemon runs makes the
    third step answer `NoAccount` every time, as it did before: the account goes only with a
    restart, and until then it cannot be used either (a sign-in ends with "This account was
    removed.", and no folder record can be written). The write that clears the folder's record
    fails the same way, so the daemon goes on holding the record of a folder it has forgotten;
    the refusal does not speak of the record there, and says to start konedrived again.
    `konedrivectl account remove` does not print the daemon's text for `NoAccount`: it says
    "there is no account <label> any more, so nothing was removed"
    (`konedrivectl/src/text/refusals.rs`), which is false in this case: the account is still
    listed, its sign-in deleted, and its folder forgotten. It printed the same before.
  - The manager's `add` — behind a development build's `DevTools.AddAccount`; nothing on a
    release build's bus adds a signed-out account, and `Accounts.SignIn` makes its account
    itself (`daemon/manager/sign_in.rs`) — takes back what it made when it fails while the
    account's objects are put on the bus. When `config.toml` cannot be written at that moment,
    only a warning is logged: the entry stays, the label stays taken by an account nothing
    shows, and it comes up as an account at the next start. An `add` that fails earlier, while
    the account's services are built (`build`), is not changed: it can still leave the account
    among the siblings, in the hub, and its directory. A `SignIn` that fails there takes its
    entry, its directory and its place among the siblings back; the same unwritable
    `config.toml` leaves the entry, which comes up as a signed-out account at the next start
    (A30).

  Why: the fix of `SY5` gives back a whole account and no more. Bringing a forgotten folder
  back means a new registration through the helper and, for a OneDrive folder, a full listing,
  which is the user's to ask for. The failures need the Secret Service to refuse a delete, or
  `config.toml` to be unwritable or edited by hand. DEBT · measured for the second step
  (`an_account_whose_removal_failed_half_way_still_takes_a_folder`, `konedrived/tests/accounts.rs`),
  *reasoned* for the rest · open.
