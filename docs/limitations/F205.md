- **F205. A removal that fails half-way leaves the account without its folder**
  (`daemon/manager.rs` `remove`, `sync/forget.rs` `unretire`; quality finding `SY5`) —
  `Accounts.Remove` forgets the folder first, then deletes the sign-in, then takes the account
  out of `config.toml`. When the second or the third step fails, the account stays and works
  again (it takes a folder, a sign-in and a mode, and can be removed again), but the first step
  is not taken back: the folder is forgotten at the helper, its read-only lock is off, and a
  OneDrive folder's tree store is deleted, so the folder cannot simply be registered as it was.
  The files stay. When the third step fails, the account is signed out as well. What follows:
  - Only the refusal says it (and the daemon's log): nothing is kept in `LastError`, so a client
    that drops the message of a failed `Remove` shows an account that lost its folder with no
    reason.
  - If `config.toml` cannot be written, the record of the folder cannot be taken out of it
    either (a plain Forget has the same weakness), so the folder may come back as recorded at
    the next registration or start, without its tree store.
  - A `config.toml` from which the account was taken by hand while the daemon runs makes the
    third step answer `NoAccount` every time: the account goes only with a restart.

  Why: the fix of `SY5` gives back a whole account and no more. Bringing a forgotten folder
  back means a new registration through the helper and, for a OneDrive folder, a full listing,
  which is the user's to ask for. Both failures need the Secret
  Service to refuse a delete, or `config.toml` to be unwritable. DEBT · measured
  (`an_account_whose_removal_failed_half_way_still_takes_a_folder`, `konedrived/tests/accounts.rs`;
  the third step by reading) · open.
