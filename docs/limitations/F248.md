- **F248. Whether a failed replacement is said again goes by a coarse reason, and the status quotes one failure**
  (`remote/materialize/replace.rs` `FailureReason`, `remote/listing/replacements.rs`
  `Replacements::record`; quality finding `RE10`) — FRAGILE, *reasoned*, open.
  A replacement that fails is retried after every cycle. It is an `update-failed` event only when
  it is news: a first failure of the file, one for a newer version, or one for another reason
  than the last. The reason is a type: no disk space, the download failed (with its errno), a
  file call failed (with its errno, or with the kind of the error where it is not the
  system's), the folder could not be opened, the task failed. So two different troubles of one
  reason are one event: a download that fails with `EIO` because the item is gone and then with
  `EIO` because the hash does not match (everything a download cannot get is `EIO`, see
  `status/activity.rs` `failure_reason`), the same errno from two different file calls, or the
  three ways the disk can be full. The words kept for the status are those of
  the first of them, until the reason or the version changes. The status note counts the files
  that wait and quotes one failure, the newest that was news; the others are in the activity
  log and the daemon's log only.
