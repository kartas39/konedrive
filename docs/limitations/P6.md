### P6. Anything that opens a placeholder downloads the whole file

- **Why:** Interception happens at open, and an open does not say who asks or how much will be read.
- **Effect:** Thumbnailers, indexers, IDEs and git commands download every placeholder they touch, a
  few at a time, so opening a large online-only folder in such a program is slow and fills the disk.
- **Stays because:** Filling at open is what covers every way of reading a file; filling on access
  was not chosen (decisions.md, "Whole-file hydration").
- **Where:** `docs/design/decisions.md`
