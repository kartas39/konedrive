- **A32. The window words the status line itself, and reads offline with a daemon that has no
  `Overall`.** Decision · measured (`app/tests/appstatustest.cpp`; the rule in
  `crates/konedrived/src/status/overall/tests.rs`; issue #199). The daemon decides an account's
  state and says why (`Folder.Overall`, `docs/design/desktop.md` §2.4), and the window and
  `konedrivectl` follow it. What a client still chooses:
  - "The KOneDrive service is not running": no daemon can say it. The window shows `offline` for
    it by itself.
  - The tray's worst state across accounts (`AppStatus::rank`, A17).
  - The wording of the window's status line, under the reasons that have no line of their own:
    the first that holds of the listing (`State` is `listing`), `Trouble` (with a capital
    letter), the user's pause, the account's own hold, what downloads and uploads, what waits to
    upload, "Up to date" — put together from `State`, `Paused`, `HeldBack` and the counts, as
    before. It is an order of words, not of states: the icon and the attention text never come
    from it. So the line and the state can speak of different things, as they did: "Paused ·
    checked 20 s ago" under a warning for a conflict.
  - A state the window does not know reads `offline`. So does none at all: a daemon of an older
    build, not restarted after an update, has no `Overall`, and every account then shows the
    offline icon over a line that may say "Up to date". The sidebar's line about the service
    being another build is what tells the user; `konedrivectl` leaves its `Overall:` line out.
  - `konedrivectl` says the trouble on its `Overall:` line only for the reasons that are about
    it (`stopped`, `trouble`, `unreachable`, `not-updated`); under another reason the trouble is
    in `sync status`'s `Last error:`.

  Where the daemon's rule differs from the window's rule before it, each accepted:
  - OneDrive out of reach is `offline` only when nothing else is wrong. With another note beside
    it (the watcher's, the lock's, the handles', the outbox's) the account is a warning for
    `trouble`; the window read `offline` whenever the sentence started with "cannot reach
    OneDrive".
  - The failed-update note is the note alone. The window took everything `LastError` said from
    the note on as the note, so the watcher's, the lock's, the handles' and the outbox's notes
    were shown in the attention text; now they are trouble, on the status line.

  Open.
