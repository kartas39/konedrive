### F300. No links, special files or extended attributes of the user's own in the folder

- **Why:** OneDrive holds files and directories and nothing else, and an item has one place.
- **Effect:** The mount refuses a symbolic link, a hard link, a device node, a FIFO, a socket and
  the setting of an extended attribute. A program that needs one of them in the folder (a build
  tree, a version-control checkout with links) fails there.
- **Stays because:** What cannot be sent would exist on this computer alone and be lost with the
  backing directory.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/mount/kernel_requests.rs`
