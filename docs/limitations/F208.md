- **F208. What the helper's bounds per uid on waiting opens and on roots leave open**
  (`konedrive-helper/src/jobs.rs` `MAX_SUSPENDED_OPENS_PER_UID`, `roots.rs` `MAX_ROOTS_PER_UID`
  and `Roots::with`, `registration.rs` `register_root`; quality findings `HE1`, `HE2`) — one uid
  can no longer hold every descriptor the helper has, nor grow `roots.json` without end. What the
  bounds do not cover:
  - **An open is charged to the file's owner, not to the opener.** A permission event carries no
    uid for the opener, and the descriptors are held for as long as the owner's daemon is
    silent. So a user who can read another user's placeholders can spend that user's 8192: while
    one large file of theirs is being filled, each further open of it waits and is counted, and
    past the bound the owner's own opens are refused `EAGAIN` until the fill ends. It needs
    files the owner made readable to the other user, and it ends by itself.
  - **The bound is per uid, with no bound for the machine.** Eight uids whose daemons answer
    nothing reach the unit's 65,536 descriptors together. One hostile user is one uid.
  - **A legitimate burst above 8192 opens waiting at once for one user's daemon is refused
    `EAGAIN`** beyond the bound. The largest measured is the VM suite's 3000.
  - **A user with more than 32 intercepted folders** is refused the 33rd (`EDQUOT`); the daemon
    shows it as the helper refusing the registration. One folder per account, so 32 accounts.
  - **Roots already in `roots.json` are not checked again.** An entry stored before the id's
    form was checked, or a uid's entries beyond 32, stay, are walked at every start, and can be
    unregistered; a log line that names such a stored id prints it escaped and cut only on the
    unregistration paths.
  - **A displaced root is unmarked only where its stored path still leads to it.** When an id is
    registered onto another directory, the old directory is found again by its path and checked
    by `(dev, ino)`, as an unregistration does (Z7). A folder replaced *at its path* by a copy,
    with the old one renamed aside, is not found: its tree keeps its marks until the helper
    restarts (opens of its placeholders answer `EIO`), and reads zeros after, like any copy of
    placeholders outside a registered folder. An old directory that was deleted lost its marks
    with its inodes. The log names the condition.
  - **Only refusals and walk failures are throttled.** A registration that succeeds still writes
    its own lines (the walk's count, a displaced root's unmarking), one set for each request; a
    request costs its sender a walk of the tree. A walk's failures are written 16 at most, then
    counted. A path in a line is printed as it is.
