- **F274. The properties the daemon announces are one table; four properties are outside it, and the
  check is by name** (`dbus/properties.rs`, `dbus/signals.rs`; quality finding `SY10`). FRAGILE,
  *measured* (`dbus::properties::tests`), open. Each property of a folder's interfaces that
  changes by itself is one row: its interface, its name and how it is read from the published
  state. Its getter answers through the row and the two emitters compare and send by the rows.
  What the table does not hold:
  - **`Source`, `IgnorePatterns`, `Thumbnails` and `MachineName`.** `Source` is the folder's
    record, not the published state: it is announced when `Path` changes, read at that moment.
    The two settings are announced by the call that sets them. `MachineName` is never announced
    (`config.toml` is read when the daemon starts). The test names the four; a fifth property
    added outside the table fails it.
  - **The test compares names, not values.** It reads each interface's own description and
    holds it against the rows. That a getter answers through the row of its own name is seen
    only where it is written (`ITEMS_LISTED.of(...)` in `items_listed`).
  - **Every getter copies the whole published state**, and the downloads under way with it, to
    read one value, as before; a `GetAll` of `Transfers` copies them twenty-one times.
  - **A property sent at once travels with its value, one message for each interface**, where
    each had a message of its own before. Two changes of the state that land before the task
    runs are sent as one, with the last value, as before.
  - **`Paused` and `PausedUntil` are two rows of one field.** A pause that only changes its end
    announces `PausedUntil` alone.
