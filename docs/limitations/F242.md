- **F242. Every change of a folder stops its sync, and a helper's reconnect starts it again with a
  Full reconcile** (`sync/folder.rs` `change`, `sync/bring_up.rs` `resume`) — the folder's state is
  changed through one door, which stops the folder's sync before it gives the state out: the
  poller, the outbox worker, the watcher, the thumbnail filler. A change that leaves the folder as
  one that syncs starts the sync again, and a started sync's first cycle is a Full reconcile, with
  a read-write folder's Full local scan before it. What follows:
  - **The helper connecting again restarts the sync of every OneDrive folder.** Before, the
    bring-up of a folder that was up nudged the running sync (one delta cycle). Now it costs a walk
    of the whole folder against the tree, once per reconnect; an upload under way is cut and its
    row replayed, as at any stop. A helper that restarts is rare, and it has lost every mark, so
    the Full reconcile is not wasted; how long it takes on a large folder was not measured.
  - **A bring-up that fails leaves the folder down, with no sync.** Before, a folder that was up
    kept its running sync when the helper's re-registration failed, placing directories the helper
    did not mark. Now `State` reads `error`, `LastError` says why, `Refresh` is refused `NotUp`,
    and the next connect tries again.
  - **A call that is refused from the state alone does not stop the sync**: a registration on an
    account whose folder is up is refused before the change begins. Every other writer stops it
    (a switch of mode, a Forget, a forced drop, a `Refresh` of a folder whose sync is not running).
  - **The reason of a Full local scan is `read-write` only for the scan the switch itself starts.**
    A switch made while the folder's sync was not running leaves the next start's scan saying
    `start` (`LocalScan.Reason`); before, a flag kept the reason until a watcher started.
