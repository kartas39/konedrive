- **D29. Items moved down a layer are still named by their old paths.** To make the daemon's
  directories use each other one way only, some items moved to a lower directory, and the
  module they left re-exports them, so that no other file had to change its path:
  `hydration::dehydrate::DehydrateError` (now in `folder/root.rs`), `upload::paused`,
  `set_paused` and `set_paused_blocking` (`conditions/running.rs`, where `paused` is
  `user_pause`), `upload::move_out::Helper` and `Linked` (`helper/linked.rs`),
  `upload::copy_name`, `machine_name` and `default_machine_name` (`local/names.rs`),
  `local::scan::LocalScan` and `ScanState`, and `remote::live::LiveChanges`
  (`status/snapshot.rs`). Each has two names until its users are changed to the new one.
  `conditions/running.rs` also has a clock of its own (`unix_now`), as `local/scan.rs` has:
  the shared one is in `status/`, above it. The order of the layers is checked by a script
  only once the guard exists (`scripts/check-structure.sh`).
