  users need.** To make the daemon's directories use each other one way only, some items
  moved to a lower directory.

    `hydration::dehydrate::DehydrateError` (now in `folder/root.rs`; its `io_error` is
    re-exported `pub(crate)`), `upload::paused`, `set_paused` and `set_paused_blocking`

    `remote::live::LiveChanges` (`status/snapshot.rs`). Each has two names until its users
    are changed to the new one.

    `local/names.rs`) has three: `local::names::…`, `upload::local::…` and `upload::…`.
