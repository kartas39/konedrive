- **D29. Some items moved down a layer sit lower than their name suggests, and one lower than
  its users need.** To make the daemon's directories use each other one way only, some items
  moved to a lower directory.
  - Their users name them where they are now, and the modules they left re-export nothing:
    `DehydrateError` and `io_error` (`folder/root.rs`, were in `hydration/dehydrate.rs`);
    `set_paused`, `set_paused_blocking` and `user_pause`, which was `upload::paused`
    (`conditions/running.rs`); `Helper` and `Linked` (`helper/linked.rs`, were in
    `upload/move_out.rs`); `LocalScan`, `ScanState` and `LiveChanges` (`status/snapshot.rs`,
    were in `local/scan.rs` and `remote/live.rs`); `copy_name`, `machine_name` and
    `default_machine_name` (`local/names.rs`, were in `upload/local.rs`).
  - `Tracked` is in `hydration/tracked.rs`, since `status/` may not name `hydration/`.
  - `DehydrateError` sits in `folder/root.rs` whole, although three of its variants
    (`NotHydrated`, `ModifiedLocally`, `InUse`) are used only by `hydration/`: moved whole so
    that no code changes.
  - `conditions/running.rs` has a clock of its own (`unix_now`), as `local/scan.rs` has: the
    shared one is in `status/`, above it.
  - The order of the layers is checked by a script only once the guard exists
    (`scripts/check-structure.sh`).
