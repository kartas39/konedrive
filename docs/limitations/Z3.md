### Z3. A hard link or a move out of the folder escapes interception

- **Why:** Coverage follows names through covered directories; the kernel has no coverage of a whole
  subtree and reports no hard link made elsewhere.
- **Effect:** A hard link outside the folder to a file not downloaded reads zeros until a full scan
  finds the file and covers it on its own. A file moved out reads zeros for about two seconds, and
  again after a reboot or helper restart until the daemon looks.
- **Stays because:** The kernel offers no subtree coverage and no event that names a new hard link.
- **Where:** `docs/design/hydration.md` §16
