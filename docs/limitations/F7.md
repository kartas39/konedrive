- **F7. Under descriptor exhaustion, a filled file can be denied `EIO`** — the helper re-read
  its state through a duplicate descriptor and could not when none was free. Never zeros.
  Closed: the helper reads a file's `fstat` and attributes through the event's own descriptor
  (`konedrive-helper/src/events.rs` `stat_of`; the readers of `konedrive-fs/src/placeholder.rs`
  take any descriptor), so deciding an open and checking a finished hydration need no
  descriptor of their own. What still needs one is the duplicate the daemon fills through
  (`jobs::Dispatch`): without it the hydration is not asked for and its openers get `EIO`, a
  file that was not filled.
