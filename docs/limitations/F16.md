### F16. A rescue fails when the sync folder is itself a mount point

- **Why:** A rescue is one rename, and a rename cannot cross filesystems.
- **Effect:** When neither the data directory nor the folder's parent is on the folder's filesystem,
  or the parent cannot be written, every cycle that needs a rescue fails. Nothing is lost and the
  status says why, until the folder is moved.
- **Stays because:** We chose a rename over a copy, because a rename cannot lose bytes.
- **Where:** `docs/design/decisions.md`, "A rescue is one rename, never a copy"
