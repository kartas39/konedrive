- **F204. A sign-in whose account is reported signed out meanwhile ends in silence**
  (`account/sign_in.rs` `commit_sign_in`; quality finding `AC1`). FRAGILE, *reasoned* (traced
  in the code; the commit's refusal is measured by
  `a_sign_in_commits_only_while_the_account_is_signing_in`), open.
  Since the fix of `AC1` a sign-in is stored only while the account still shows `signing-in`.
  A cancel, a sign-out and a newer sign-in end that state under the session lock and supersede
  the attempt. One thing ends it otherwise: a token refresh that Microsoft answers
  `invalid_grant` (`konedrive-graph/src/token.rs`, which deletes the stored token and calls
  `StateHandle::signed_out`, `account/state.rs`). It sets `signed-out` and "Session expired.
  Sign in again." without the session lock and without superseding the attempt, whatever the
  state was.
  - **When it can happen:** the account is signing in while a refresh token that no longer
    works is still in its wallet item (a wallet that did not answer at startup leaves an
    account signed out with its token stored), and something asks that account for an access
    token meanwhile: another account's sign-in, which asks every account with no drive
    recorded for its drive (`settle_siblings`, then `learn_drive`), or a token export
    (`TokenExport`, development builds).
  - **What the user sees:** the browser page says the sign-in is done (the listener answers
    before the commit), the account stays signed out with "Session expired. Sign in again.",
    and nothing says that the sign-in just made was dropped. The attempt goes on waiting for
    the browser after the state changed; the daemon's log has a warning when the commit
    refuses. Before the fix the account was signed in.
  - **What helps:** signing in again; the dead token is gone by then.
  - Kept, because the refusal is what `AC1` asks for: an account told that its sign-in ended
    is not signed in afterwards. Removing it means ending the attempt where the refresh
    reports the sign-out (the cancel channel and the generation, under the session lock),
    which the token manager cannot reach today.
