### F304. A name OneDrive holds with an item the folder cannot show cannot be used here

- **Why:** OneDrive keeps one item per name in a directory, whatever the case of its letters, and
  an item the folder never shows (P7: a OneNote notebook, the Personal Vault, a shared folder)
  holds its name all the same.
- **Effect:** Making a file or a directory under such a name, or renaming to it, also in another
  case, answers "file exists" though nothing is shown there. The activity log names the name and
  what holds it, once while the folder is mounted, and says to rename that item in OneDrive.
- **Stays because:** The other choice is to take the name here and rename the item afterwards,
  behind the user's back, as the sync does for two items of one name.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/mount/item_write_operations.rs`
