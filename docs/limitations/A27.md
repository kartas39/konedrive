- **A27. An account left under the label "Signing in…" by an older window is not removed any
  more.** LIMIT · reasoned. Before `Accounts.SignIn` the window added a new account under the
  temporary label "Signing in…" and removed it itself, at its next start too when it had crashed in
  the middle. The window no longer looks for that label, and the daemon marks its own drafts by a
  field of `config.toml`, which such an account does not have. One left by a window that crashed
  before the upgrade stays in the switcher, signed out, until the user removes it (**Remove
  Account…**, or `konedrivectl account remove "Signing in…"`). Open.
