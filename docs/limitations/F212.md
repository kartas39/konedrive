- **F212. Some failures of the tree store inside a reconcile still do not stop the folder**
  (`remote/materialize/rw/leaving.rs`, `remote/listing/rw.rs`, `remote/listing.rs` `record`;
  quality findings `RE6`, `RE1`) — a store failure that ends a cycle is `CycleError::Store`
  where a call of the cycle's own or the materializer meets it: `RootState` reads `error` and
  the write gate is closed until a cycle goes through. These are not that:
  - the leaving walk turns a store failure into an `io::Error` (`leaving_at`), which is logged,
    and the row is passed over until the next cycle; a store failure inside the examination
    before a folder goes (`keeps_leaving`) ends the cycle, but as the trouble that is only said
    and tried again;
  - the read-write reconcile only warns when it cannot settle what a `404` blocked, let the
    outbox wait for folders made again, or drop removals of items already gone;
  - `record` only warns when it cannot count what was listed, and the event says 0.

  A store that fails there usually fails at the commit too, which stops the folder; one that
  fails only there is seen in the log alone. They wait for the leaving mechanism's rewrite
  (`docs/tasks/quality-plan.md`, B4). DEBT · reasoned · planned (B4).
