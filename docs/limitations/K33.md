- **K33. What the menu's answer does not say.** DEBT · reasoned, except where a test is named.
  `Files.Menu` restates what `Pin`, `Unpin` and `FreeUp` check before they change anything, and
  no more:
  - "Free up space" is disabled with a reason (`free-up-why`), and one reason only: the first of
    `FreeUp`'s own checks that does not hold, in its order — the helper, a folder above, a change
    waiting to be uploaded. A selection refused for two of them shows the first, and the next
    only once that one is dealt with. Over several accounts' folders it is the first account's that
    refuses.
  - `not-uploaded` comes from the tree store's read-only connection, which sees what was last
    committed: a change being recorded at that moment is not in the answer, the entry is enabled,
    and the click is refused `NotUploaded` by `FreeUp`, which asks the writer
    (`the_outbox_is_listed_decided_on_and_its_files_are_not_freed_up` holds the two against each
    other at rest). The question reads the outbox's rows once for the whole selection, whatever
    their number, where `FreeUp` asks by index for each file.
  - The tooltips of the three new reasons are shortened from what `dolphin/src/refusaltext.cpp`
    says for the same refusal of `FreeUp`, by hand: two texts for one refusal, which can drift
    (the texts are point 4 of issue #199). A reason the plugin does not know disables the entry
    with no tooltip.
  - What a free-up finds only as it frees is not anticipated: a file in use, one changed here, and
    a helper that is running but not connected in a folder registered without interception
    (`NoHelper` at the first file). The entry is enabled, and the click is refused or counts the
    file as kept, as before.
  - "Open in OneDrive" is `disabled` only for an item with no id. An account that is signed out
    still shows it enabled, and the click is refused `NotSignedIn`.
  - An account's folder itself is offered "Open in OneDrive" alone, although `Pin`, `Unpin` and
    `FreeUp` take that path too: the answer follows what the menu offered before, not the calls.
  - The keys and their values are spelled by hand on both sides (`dbus/org.konedrive.Files.xml`,
    `dolphin/src/syncclient.cpp`), and no test runs the plugin against the real daemon: a value
    spelled differently on one side hides the entry. open.
