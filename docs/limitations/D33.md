- **D33. What the one `send` of `konedrive-graph` left as it was, and what it rests on**
  (`konedrive-graph/src/drive/`; quality findings `GR1`, `GR2`, `GR6`). DEBT, *reasoned*,
  open.
  - **A fragment's retry is not a value of `Throttle`.** `send` has two policies (`Wait` for
    reads, `Return` for writes). A fragment is sent with `Return`, and `upload_chunk` waits,
    asks the session where it stands and sends again: its retry also covers a request that
    got no answer, and can end with what a write ends with (`SessionGone`, where the session
    stands), which `send` does not know.
  - **An error prints its message only, with `{:?}` too** (`Detail`). The status and Graph's
    code it carries are read through `DriveError::status` and `code`, and the fields of
    `Detail`; they are left out of `Debug` so that no log line changed when they were added.
  - **A read that is refused now reads the answer's body** for Graph's code (`get_json`),
    within the request's 60 seconds: before, the body of a refusal other than `410` was not
    read. A `404` and the other calls (content, a download, a thumbnail) still do not.
  - **A write whose token is rejected twice is an error before its body is read.** Before,
    the second `401` went through `error_from`, where Graph's codes `nameAlreadyExists` and
    `quotaLimitReached` decide whatever the status; a `401` with one of them is not known to
    exist.
  - **`DriveClient::profile` and `drive` tell the client's transfer pool of a `429` or a
    `503`.** The account asks them through a client of its own, whose pool nothing else
    uses; a client that shares the account's pool (`with_pool`) would throttle it.
  - **`GET /me/drive` has three bodies**: `drive_id` needs the id, `quota` neither, `drive`
    the quota. One body would change which answer each of them refuses.
  - **`SESSION_EXPIRED` and `WALLET_LOCKED` are still in the crate** (`token.rs`). Moving
    them means a `RefreshReport` that says what happened in place of the sentence, and the
    crate's tests assert the sentence through it.
