- **F276. A stale drive that cannot be taken off an empty folder stays, and the folder is registered all the same** (`folder/root.rs`,
  `drive_of`, `forget_drive`, `mark_drive`; `sync/bring_up.rs`, `register`; quality finding
  `HY10`) — a folder that carries another account's drive (`user.konedrive.drive`) and is
  empty may be registered: the stale drive is taken off first. The read and the removal are
  two functions now (`drive_allows` did both and answered a `bool`), with what they leave
  as it was:
  - the removal's failure is logged and the registration goes on; `mark_drive` then writes
    nothing, since the folder carries a drive, and the folder keeps the other account's.
    The next registration of that folder by this account, once it holds files, is refused
    `ForeignFolder`;
  - the folder is looked at twice (empty when read, empty when the drive is taken off), and
    a file put into it in between is not noticed by the first look: the removal checks
    again and leaves the drive, and the registration's own "must be empty" check refuses a
    folder without a root id.
