- **F262. An account that is added is on the bus a moment before its folder is one of the
  daemon's** (`daemon/manager.rs` `add`, `list`) — `Accounts.Add` puts the account's objects on
  the bus and only then lists the account, in the manager's list and in the registry of folders
  (`sync/registry.rs`), so that `Accounts.List` never names an account that is not on the bus.
  A call on the new account's own objects in between finds a folder service that is not yet one
  of the daemon's: it runs on the default hold settings, and a helper that connects in that
  moment does not resume it. Nothing is lost: the account has no folder yet, a folder needs a
  sign-in first, and being listed tells the service the helper's state, the hold's settings and
  the machine's conditions of that moment. Before, the service put itself on the hub's list as it
  was made, before it was on the bus, and the manager took it off again when the export failed.
