### Z5. Freeing a file's space while it is still exempt leaves zeros forever

- **Why:** The exemption from interception survives any change to the file, so nothing notices that
  the content is gone.
- **Effect:** Every place that frees a file's space must first have the helper lift the exemption
  and must stop on any failure. A slip here is silent and permanent.
- **Stays because:** Without surviving changes the kernel cannot place the exemption at all while
  the daemon has the file open for writing.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/hydration/dehydrate.rs`
