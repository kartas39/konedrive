- **D34. Outbox reasons and local skips are types over the strings they were.** DEBT,
  *measured* (`konedrivectl`'s `text::uploads::tests::every_reason_is_stored_under_its_spelling_and_is_worded`,
  the tests of `konedrive-reason`, `konedrive-tree`'s `outbox::encoded::tests`), open. A row's
  reason is a `Reason`, what an examination never uploads a `LocalSkip`, both in the crate
  `konedrive-reason` (no dependencies; `konedrive-tree::outbox` re-exports it), with
  the stored spelling, the detail and the group of each; `konedrive-tree/src/outbox/encoded.rs`
  reads the forms of a row's `target_name` (quality findings
  `X1`, `UP2`, `UP3`, `TR4`, `CL3`). No reason stored or sent changed, so what the strings were
  is still there:
  - **The spellings.** Four keys are whole sentences (`changed in OneDrive again and again`,
    `changing in OneDrive again and again`, `the upload session ended twice`, `not allowed now`),
    and a detail stands behind its key in two ways: `<key>: <detail>` and
    `too-big:<needs>:<free>`. The database holds them, and the window's and Dolphin's tables
    (`app/uploadreasons.cpp`, the Dolphin plugins) are keyed by them; nothing checks those
    tables against the enum.
  - **A string no variant spells** (an older version's raw error text, a damaged row, a key
    that takes no detail with one behind it, sizes not written as the daemon writes them) is
    `Reason::Other` or `LocalSkip::Other`: kept, written back and shown as it is. Its key and
    its group are still cut from the string as before (`key_of`: the part before the first
    `: ` when that is a key of either table), so `network: …` is summed under `network`
    though it is no `Reason::Network`. `Other` can be built by any code, and one built from
    a known spelling is not equal to the variant: only `parse` makes one.
  - **The two tables share one key space.** A summary's key says nothing of whether it is a
    row's reason or a skip; `known_group` looks in the reasons first, then in the skips.
    `not-downloaded` is in both (`Reason::NotLocal`, `LocalSkip::NotDownloaded`), with one
    group and one sentence (`A24`).
  - **`Reason::ALL` and `LocalSkip::ALL` are written by hand.** A variant left out of them
    is not read back from its spelling (it becomes `Other`). The test in `konedrivectl` does
    not compile until a new variant is spelled there, and counts the lists (49 and 11); it
    cannot see a variant missing from the list by itself.
  - **`konedrivectl` has no sentence for fourteen row reasons** (`not-found`,
    `changed-while-sending`, `parent-not-in-onedrive`, `hash-mismatch`, `move-out-not-yet`,
    `waiting-for-the-helper`, `moved-out-unreachable`, `back-in-the-folder`,
    `moved-out-place-unknown`, `download-failed`, `gone-once`,
    `handle-from-another-filesystem`, `gone-unproved`, `lease-probe-failed`) **and for
    `ignored`**: it shows them as stored, as it did. The test names them, so a sentence added
    for one is a change made on purpose.
  - **`detail()` has no caller** outside the tests: what stands behind a key is read from
    the variant where it is needed.
  - **`ignored`** is a key of the skips that no code writes; it is kept because the table of
    groups had it.
  - **The upgrade of an older row** (`schema/migrations.rs`, `hash-mismatch:<item id>`) writes
    the key in its SQL, not through the enum: a step is not changed after it is written.
  - **`target_name` is still a text column with three meanings** (a name, a temporary name,
    or a `move-out` row's absolute path), told apart by what the text begins with
    (`swap_name`, `last_place`). The field of `OutboxRow` stays a public string, which the
    store's own code, the examination's detections and the tests still set. A row's snapshot
    is typed and has columns of its own since schema 6 (`D36`).
  - **What goes over D-Bus is strings**: `Changes()`, `NotUploaded()`, `NotUploadedSummary()`
    and `NotUploadedFiles(reason)` take and give the spellings, and `kept_back` works on
    keys as text where it meets them.
