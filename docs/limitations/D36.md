### D36. A store of another version, or a lost one, is rebuilt

- **Why:** Every change of the store's layout has a number, and a build reads only the layout of
  its own number. Only the layout just before it, when the change added to it and took nothing
  away, is brought to today's in place.
- **Effect:** After an upgrade over several layouts or one that changed what was there, a
  downgrade, or the loss of the store's file, OneDrive is listed again in full and the rows are made
  again from the backing directory. Lost with the old store: the pins, the executable bits, the
  activity log, the conflict list, open upload sessions, and every unsent change that left nothing
  on disk (a removal, a move).
- **Stays because:** The store is a map that can be made again from OneDrive and the disk; a
  migration step for every layout ever shipped would be code that runs once and can lose data when
  it is wrong.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrive-tree/src/schema/old_store.rs`
