- **A31. Calls on a draft's objects are answered, not refused by a rule of their own.** FRAGILE ·
  reasoned. A draft is on the bus, and the `ObjectManager` announces it, so a generic tool can call
  it; konedrive's clients are given no reason to. What happens is the simplest safe thing, not a
  design: its folder takes nothing (`Folder.Register` answers `Failed`, "being removed");
  `Accounts.Remove` of its path, `Account.SignOut` and `Accounts.SetClientId` end it as
  `cancelled`; `Account.SetLabel` is accepted, and the label is overwritten by the email when the
  sign-in succeeds. None of these calls on a draft has a test of its own. Open.
