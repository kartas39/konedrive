- **A31. Calls on a draft's objects are answered, not refused by a rule of their own.** FRAGILE ·
  reasoned. A draft is on the bus, and the `ObjectManager` announces it, so a generic tool can call
  it; konedrive's clients are given no reason to. What happens is the simplest safe thing, not a
  design: its folder takes nothing (`Folder.Register` answers `Failed`, "being removed");
  `Accounts.Remove` of its path and `Account.SignOut` end it as `cancelled`, and so does an
  `Accounts.SetClientId` that is not refused: refused (an account is signed in or signing in, or
  the ID is not one), it leaves the draft as it is. `Account.SetLabel` is accepted, and the label
  is overwritten by the email when the sign-in succeeds. Between a sign-in stored (the token is in
  the wallet) and the draft listed, a `SignIn`, a `SetClientId` or a `Remove` that comes first
  still ends the draft as `cancelled`: the sign-in that had succeeded is lost, and the user signs
  in again; a wallet that refuses the delete then keeps a token with no account. None of these
  calls on a draft has a test of its own. Open.
