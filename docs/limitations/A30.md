- **A30. A daemon that stops while it makes the account of a sign-in leaves what it had made.**
  LIMIT · reasoned. Nothing exists for a new account until its browser sign-in has succeeded; then
  the daemon makes it in this order: the entry in `config.toml` (the label, the drive and the
  `login_hint`, one write), `accounts/<id>/`, the token stored, the account listed, its objects on
  the bus, `SignInFinished`. Nothing is taken back at the next start, so a daemon that stops in the
  middle leaves:
  - after the entry and before the token: a signed-out account under its final label, listed at
    the next start. The user signs it in again (`konedrivectl login`, **Sign in** on its page) or
    removes it;
  - after the token: the whole account, signed in, at the next start.

  Never a token that no account owns. In both cases no `SignInFinished` was sent: the window and
  `konedrivectl account add` say that the service stopped (A28), although the account may be
  there when it is back. Open.
