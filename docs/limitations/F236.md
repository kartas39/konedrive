- **F236. What the upload worker's waits leave: the throttle's rules are reasoned, its note is not said again after a closed gate, and two looks wait for a wake**
  (`upload/engine/state.rs` `Throttle`, `Trouble`; `upload/engine.rs` `next_due`, `ask_gate`,
  `publish`; `upload/engine/drain.rs`; `status/snapshot.rs` `OutboxNote::after_worker`;
  `sync/outbox.rs` `Host::status`; quality findings `UP4`, `UP10`). FRAGILE and PROVISIONAL,
  *reasoned*, open.
  - **The throttle's rules were not measured against OneDrive.** A time OneDrive names takes
    the place of a wait the worker chose, even a shorter one; of two named times the later
    end stands; a refusal that names none during a wait adds nothing, so rows refused
    together are one throttle (10 s, doubling with each throttle that names no time).
  - **What the folder's `LastError` says of the worker's waits, and when it does not.**
    "OneDrive asked to slow down; uploads continue at HH:MM" (the wall clock's minute the wait
    is over in, rounded up) for a wait of 5 s or more (`THROTTLE_SAID`, a number chosen, not
    measured): a shorter one is not said at all. "the folder cannot be opened (<error>);
    uploads wait" while a drain could not open a folder that is still there; it stands over
    the throttle's. Both are said only where no other note of the outbox is. A closed write
    gate says its own over them, and when it opens while the throttle still lasts, the
    throttle is not said again: the gate is asked by a row between two fragments, the worker
    itself asks nothing while it is throttled, and nothing hands the status over again. The
    rows are `ready` and go when the wait is over.
  - **A folder that cannot be opened is looked at again at the next wake, or after five
    minutes.** The worker takes nothing until a drain opens the folder, and the note above
    stays until then. A folder that is gone stops the sync and the worker with it (the
    watcher's root-gone hook, `sync/watcher.rs`), and the sync that starts when it is back
    builds a new worker; the wait is for a failure the watcher does not take for "gone" (no
    permission, no descriptor left, an I/O error), where nothing tells the worker that it
    passed.
  - **The quota's automatic read waits for a look with no row in flight.** The read is one
    request awaited by the loop itself, so it is made only then, as before this change
    (it was made when a drain began). While uploads follow each other without a gap, a read
    that is due waits for the gap.
  - **One more asking of the write gate at every wait.** While rows are in flight the drain
    also wakes when something falls due (`next_due`), which asks the write gate (one section
    on a blocking thread, one read of `config.toml`), once for each event of the drain.
    Not measured.
  - **A gate that opens wakes the worker only through what opened it.** Every reason the
    gate has to be closed but one is also a reason for the account to run read-only
    (`AccountService::recompute_mode`), which stops the worker; the return to read-write
    builds a new one. The one left, the sync stopped by blocking trouble, wakes the worker
    when a cycle clears it (`Writes::reopened`). Traced by reading. A new reason for the gate
    that is neither would be noticed only by the five-minute look.
  - **A stopped worker hands its host nothing more** (`Engine::silence`): a task that
    outlives the stop (the detached `cycle_done`) cannot put back the counts or the note the
    host cleared after it. The stop sets this after the worker's task has ended; a hand-over
    that began before it is finished first. By reading; no test.
  - **Two keys of a failure have no test of their own.** `local-error` and `index-error` are
    what a step's file error and store error are stored as (`outcome_of`); only `network` and
    `upload-error` are checked on a stored row (`upload/tests/mod.rs`). The table of keys is
    checked in `konedrive-reason`.
  - **One test waits a real throttle out**:
    `sync::tests::onedrive::read_write::a_throttle_is_said_in_last_error_until_it_ends` asks
    for 5 s and gives the note 6 s to come and 30 s to go. Not seen failing.
