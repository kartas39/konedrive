- **A25. The account's own hold shows as paused, and the tray lifts it for every account.**
  Decision · measured (`appstatustest::aHeldAccountIsPaused`,
  `appstatustest::theTraySyncsAnywayEveryHeldAccount`; issue #54). An account whose `HeldBack` is not
  empty is in the "paused" state, ranked as the user's pause (the daemon's rule since issue #199:
  `Folder.Overall` reads `paused`, `held-back`), and its status line says why with the
  Status page's words ("Paused: metered connection"); the user's pause, when both are on, is what the
  line says. The hold's texts live twice, in `AccountStatus::heldBackText` and in `StatusPage.qml`,
  kept equal by hand; an unknown reason reads "Paused by itself" in both. The tray's "Sync Anyway",
  shown while any account is held and not paused by the user, calls `SyncAnyway` on each such
  account — the whole app's action, as `konedrivectl sync anyway --all`, while the Status page's
  stays per account (write design §11). Open.
