- **F270. The two downloads share their checks, not their loop** (`hydration/source/download.rs`,
  `hydration/source/parts.rs`, `hydration/source/guards.rs`; quality finding `HY3`) — the
  refusal of a wrong offset and of a declared size of 0, the count of breaks and the pause,
  the acceptance of a checkpoint, the hash at the end and the one start over are one copy
  each in `guards.rs`. The loop that reads a stream, writes at an offset and counts towards
  a checkpoint is still written twice: once for one stream, in file order, and once for a
  piece of a download in parts. The two differ in what they count (bytes written so far
  against the gap-free start of several pieces) and in how they write (alone against
  beside), and a download in one stream is not a download in parts with one piece: it does
  not know the size before its first answer and takes whatever the source serves. What can
  still drift is therefore small: the buffer handed into a section, and where a checkpoint
  is due. DEBT · reasoned · open.
