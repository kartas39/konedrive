### F80. A finishing upload can replace an edit just made in OneDrive

- **Why:** OneDrive checks the version guard when an upload session is opened, not when it
  completes.
- **Effect:** An edit made in OneDrive between our last look at the item and the last fragment is
  replaced by the upload. It stays in the item's version history, but no conflict copy is made.
- **Stays because:** OneDrive offers no guard on the completing request; we can only narrow the
  window.
- **Where:** `docs/design/writes.md` §6.3
