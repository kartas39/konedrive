### F120. A placeholder moved out of the folder reads zeros until marked again

- **Why:** Interception follows marked directories, and the kernel has no mark that follows a file
  out of them.
- **Effect:** A not-downloaded file moved out alone reads zeros for about two seconds after the
  move, and after a reboot or a helper restart until the upload worker looks. A move made while the
  daemon is not running stays uncovered until it starts.
- **Stays because:** Covering it would mean intercepting the whole filesystem or marking every
  placeholder one by one, which we chose not to pay for.
- **Where:** `docs/limitations/Z3.md`
