### F175. No automatic pause without NetworkManager, UPower or power-profiles-daemon

- **Why:** The metered, battery and power-profile states are read only from those three system
  services.
- **Effect:** On a machine without one of them, that reason never pauses the sync, whatever the
  settings say; only the journal says so. A service that starts after the daemon is noticed only at
  its first change or the daemon's next start.
- **Stays because:** The desktop offers no other common source for this information, and guessing a
  hold would stop syncing without cause.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/conditions/mod.rs`
