- **D52. The fixture of `remote/`: a step is a cycle of a new `Listing`, and a read-only folder a test writes into is unlocked between steps.** DEBT,
  *reasoned*, open. Quality task B4-7 made `World::step` (`remote/testing.rs`; `listed_as`,
  `changed`, `changed_full`) hand what OneDrive says to the cycle's own staging
  (`Listing::stage`, `remote/listing/stage.rs`): the stale-delta guard, what is staged again and
  the choice of the scope are the daemon's. What is still the fixture's:
  - **The fetch.** A test gives tree rows in place of Graph's pages (`Says::Whole`,
    `Says::Delta`), with a link the fixture makes up. Only `Says::Fetched` and `World::cycle`
    ask the fake OneDrive.
  - **Each step is a cycle of a `Listing` of its own.** It has no cycle before it: every outbox
    commit of the store is looked at again (`revisit_from` is 0), no replacement is started
    after it, and whether the cycle was asked for a Full reconcile is what the test says, not
    what the cycle before left.
  - **A delta that changes nothing reconciles nothing**, as in the daemon: a test that wants
    the folder looked at with no change says `Says::DeltaInFull`.
  - **An unlocked read-only folder does not exist in the daemon.** `Options::locked: false`
    without `writes` (`remote/materialize/tests.rs` `world(false)`, 17 tests) is a read-only
    folder reconciled under the lock, whose lock the fixture takes off after every step and
    cycle (`Disk::unlock_tree`), so that the test can write into it with `std::fs`. Each such
    reconcile therefore starts on a tree that was unlocked behind the daemon's back: what
    these tests say of file modes is what a Full reconcile repairs, not what a folder kept
    locked shows. `World::read_only` and `world(true)` leave the lock on.
  - **The read-only enumeration** (`remote/listing/rw/tests/stale/read_only.rs`) takes its
    listings from the read-write one and varies what was done here over two states only:
    nothing, and one downloaded file changed. A file made in the folder, an object of another
    account and a download under way are not in it.
