- **D53. The enumeration of local acts is held to a small model of the rule, a second writing
  of it; what the examination's four parts left as it was.** DEBT, *reasoned*, open.
  - `local/tests/acts.rs` decides the outcome a combination must have with a small model of the
    folder's objects (which one carries which id, which one the listing placed) and the rule of
    F53, and with nothing read from the store: what the store knows before the first look is the
    model's (every placed object on record, or none), and what it knows after it is what the
    model says that look found. The model is a second, small writing of the rule; where both are
    wrong the same way the test says nothing. For a Full scan interrupted by the second act the
    model takes the folder's own directory as it was before that act and what is below as it is
    after it: right for this folder, whose second acts come at the first directory read.
  - The first look is held to its removing rows only (`delete`, `move`, `move-out`: none but
    what the model calls for from what that look listed), to the disk and to the marks; the
    look after it to the full row list, the list, Activity and the record of every item found.
  - A first look with nothing on record takes the object at the item's place for the item and
    records it; from the second look on that record is the evidence the rule asks for. So after a
    rebuilt store an empty copy beside its original is listed by the first look and removed by the
    second (it held nothing), and a file deleted between the two is a `delete`.
  - Its fake helper answers "alive at this path" for every object that exists when both acts are
    made, in the folder or in the directory beside it, and "gone" for anything else; it always
    answers, and no row is being sent. What leaves the folder leaves in the first act: a file,
    a folder with one file, a file out of its folder. A second act never moves anything.
  - A batch of names is modelled as the examination reads it: each name, and the whole of a
    directory in which a named name is not there. Two acts move an object where such a batch
    does not look (`a.txt` into `docs`, `docs/f.txt` into `keep`); nothing else is hidden from
    a look. A folder holds one item, so "the folder waits for the least settled item in it" is
    enumerated with one item only, and never with an item that was held back on its own before
    its folder was asked after (`a_subfolder_dragged_out_that_is_held_back_keeps_its_parent_from_being_deleted`
    is the test of that).
  - The parts of a run are fields of one `Run`, and every step is still a method on it: the
    compiler does not hold a step to the parts it should use. Entries are read by reference (the
    listing is borrowed for the whole run) and never edited. Rows are not addressed by a typed
    index: nothing outside `Rows` addresses a row by index any more.
  - `missing_item`, `removal` and `left_before` (`local/examine/missing.rs`) still ask after one
    another's items. What ends it is one rule, written at `Decisions::settle` and held by an
    `open(id)` check at the head of `missing_item` and of `removal`, and in `left_before` for
    each child before it is asked after; nothing in the types holds a new caller to it.
