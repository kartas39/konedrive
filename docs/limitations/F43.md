### F43. All accounts of one user share one link to the helper

- **Why:** The helper sends a user's opens to that user's newest connection only.
- **Effect:** The 64 opens the helper lets wait at once are shared by all the user's accounts, so
  64 opens waiting in one account hold up an open in another. Each account downloads through its
  own pool. After a reconnect the folders come up one after another before any open is served.
- **Stays because:** Telling accounts apart would put routing into the helper, which we keep small.
- **Where:** `crates/konedrived/src/helper/hub.rs`
