- **F246. An item that cannot stay in the folder and has not left it yet is on the skipped
  list, with nothing that says it is still here** (`konedrive-tree/src/query.rs` `skipped`,
  `counts`; `dbus/org.konedrive.Folder.xml` `Skipped`) — an item OneDrive has under a name or of a
  kind the folder cannot hold, whose change still waits in `deferred` while the base places it
  here, is listed by `Skipped()` and counted in `SkippedCount` from the cycle that learnt of it, as
  OneDrive has it: its path there, and the reason. `Skipped()` is still `a(ss)`: the line does not
  say that the item is still on this computer, where, or what it waits for. Today such an item is
  rare (one moved or deleted here and not examined yet when OneDrive renamed it); the part of `B4`
  that makes every such item wait (`docs/tasks/b4-design.md`, part 6) adds what it waits for to
  the line, on every side of the bus. A folder that waits and an item inside it that waits too
  are two lines, not one, and the inner one's path names the folder as the base has it, not as
  OneDrive does: the folders above a waiting row are read from `items`. A waiting row an outbox
  commit superseded is left out, as `live_deferred` would drop it. Until its change is taken, the item is counted both in
  `ItemsPlaced` and in `SkippedCount`. The count is walked once per cycle that changed the tree
  (F167): an item that begins to wait through an outbox commit is counted by the cycle after it.
