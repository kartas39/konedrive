The first release of KOneDrive: a OneDrive client for KDE Plasma, with Windows' "Files On-Demand"
and no FUSE. Your OneDrive is a folder of real files at their real sizes, and a file downloads the
moment something opens it.

This is an alpha. It works day to day on the author's machines; expect rough edges, and keep a
copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

## What it does

- **Files on demand**: every file is there from the start and takes no space until it is opened.
- **Always keep on this device** and **Free up space**, for files and folders.
- **Uploads**: what you change, move or delete in the folder goes back to OneDrive. Off until you
  turn it on for an account; until then the folder is read-only.
- **Several accounts**, each with its own folder.
- **Conflicts lose nothing**: a file changed on both sides is kept twice, and listed.
- **Dolphin**: emblems, a right-click menu, and previews of images and videos without downloading.
- **A window and a tray icon** per account, notifications and transfer progress in Plasma.
- **Pause**, by hand or by itself on a metered connection or on battery.
- **`konedrivectl`**: everything the window does, from the command line.

## Install

Fedora 44 or 45 with KDE Plasma 6, x86_64. Download the two packages for your Fedora — `.fc44` or
`.fc45` in the name, `konedrive` and `konedrive-kde` — and, in the directory they are in:

```
sudo dnf install ./konedrive-*.rpm
```

Then open **KOneDrive** from the launcher, sign in and choose an empty folder. The packages are not
signed. `SHA256SUMS` lists their checksums.

## Good to know

- Personal Microsoft accounts only; work and school accounts are not supported yet.
- A small helper runs as root to intercept the opens; it has no network and no credentials
  (`SECURITY.md`).
- What does not work and cannot simply be fixed is listed in `docs/limitations/`.
