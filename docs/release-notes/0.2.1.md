The window is laid out around one OneDrive at a time, and the packages are split: `konedrive` is
the service and the command line, `konedrive-kde` the window and the Dolphin plugins.

This is still an alpha. Keep a copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0

Download both packages and install them together, as below: the window has moved from `konedrive`
into `konedrive-kde`, and `dnf` refuses the new `konedrive` alone while the `konedrive-kde` of
0.2.0 is installed. Your accounts, folders and settings stay as they are.

## What changed

- **The window shows one OneDrive.** The switcher at the top of the sidebar picks it, and its pages
  follow: **Status**, **Issues** and **OneDrive Settings**, then the app's **Settings**. Activity
  and Account are gone: what they showed is on Status and OneDrive Settings. Refresh Now is
  **Sync Now**, and Remove Account… is **Disconnect This OneDrive…**.
- **Status** is what the folder does now, with **Sync Now**, **Pause** and **Open Folder** at the
  top; the space in one bar as long as your OneDrive's storage, with how many items are always kept
  on this computer and their size, **Free Up Space…** and **Unpin All…**; a card for each way, with
  a chart of the last two minutes; and **History**, with a search by name or folder.
- **Issues** is one list of what Not Uploaded, Conflicts and Not in the Folder showed: the changes
  that can't upload, the files kept in both versions, and what can't be shown in the folder.
- **OneDrive Settings** has **Two-way sync** as a switch, **Use Another Folder…** in place of
  Forget Folder, and the free space to keep, which takes a number only after **Edit**, so that
  scrolling the page cannot change it.
- **Adding a OneDrive**: **Add Another OneDrive…** in the switcher opens the browser at once, and
  once you are signed in the dialog that picks the folder opens by itself. A folder with anything
  in it, or another account's, is refused with why, and the dialog asks again; **Choose Folder…**
  on Status opens it later.
- **A folder that stopped** says why on Status, with **Try Again** and **Use Another Folder…**.
- **A notification** opens the window where it is dealt with, and a click on the tray icon opens
  Status.
- `konedrivectl sync status` says how much the files always kept on this device take.
- **`konedrive` needs nothing of KDE or Qt**, so the service and `konedrivectl` install without
  Plasma. On Plasma you install both packages, as before.

## Install

Fedora 44 or 45 with KDE Plasma 6, x86_64. Download the two packages for your Fedora — `.fc44` or
`.fc45` in the name, `konedrive` and `konedrive-kde` — and, in the directory they are in:

```
sudo dnf install ./konedrive-*.rpm
```

Then open **KOneDrive** from the launcher, sign in and choose an empty folder. The packages are not
signed. `SHA256SUMS` lists their checksums.

## Good to know

- Personal Microsoft accounts only; work and school accounts are not supported yet.
- What does not work and cannot simply be fixed is listed in `docs/limitations/`.
