The window says when a newer release of KOneDrive is out, the foot of its sidebar names the version
again, and a name OneDrive keeps for something the folder can't show is refused at once.

This is still an alpha. Keep a copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

## Upgrading from 0.2.1

Download both packages and install them together, as below. Your accounts, folders and settings
stay as they are.

## What changed

- **The foot of the sidebar** names the version you run again, on every page, as in 0.2.0; in
  0.2.1 it was only at the end of Settings, under About, which is gone.
- **A newer release** is said there: **Update available: 0.2.3** opens its page on GitHub, where
  its packages are, and a notification says so once. The window asks GitHub once a day; nothing is
  downloaded or installed by itself.
- **A name OneDrive keeps for something the folder can't show**, such as a OneNote notebook, is
  refused at once: creating or renaming to it, also in another case, says the name exists, and a
  notification says what holds it and to rename that in OneDrive. Before, the rename seemed to work
  and the folder then got another name by itself.

## Install

Fedora 44 or 45 with KDE Plasma 6, x86_64. Download the two packages for your Fedora — `.fc44` or
`.fc45` in the name, `konedrive` and `konedrive-kde` — and, in the directory they are in:

```
sudo dnf install ./konedrive-*.rpm
```

Then open **KOneDrive** from the launcher, sign in and choose an empty folder. The packages are not
signed. `SHA256SUMS` lists their checksums.

## Good to know

- Personal Microsoft accounts only; work and school accounts are not supported yet.
- What does not work and cannot simply be fixed is listed in `docs/limitations/`.
